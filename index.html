Their Story

As told by his college friends (embellished for fun)

💘

Before 2015 · Already taken

The story started a few years before any of us showed up. Already a couple. We are merely the supporting cast.

🎓

2015 · Enter the college gang

We met PC. His opening line, within minutes: “I have a girlfriend.” Nobody had asked.

📸

Operation Set-Up PC

Every matchmaking attempt by the gang failed. Reason: he kept showing us Neha's photo. Case closed.

📞

11 pm, every night

“Sorry, can't talk, I'm on a call.” Every plan we made came with a permanent 11 pm exception.

📝

The exam-date department

Neha seemed to know our exam dates better than we did. Honestly, we should have hired her as the class reminder.

✈️

The Australia chapter

One year apart, a different time zone, good-morning and good-night texts crossing the Indian Ocean. PC became an expert in time-zone maths and in the cost of international calls.

🏡

Back together

The distance ended. The calling plan was cancelled. The gang cheered, loudly.

🔥
