    Rasmein

    The rituals, in one line each, so nothing surprises you.

    Before the wedding
    1. I

      Haldi हल्दी

      Family rubs a turmeric paste on the bride and groom for blessings and glow. Wear something you do not mind ruining.

    2. II

      Mehendi मेहंदी

      Henna is drawn on the hands and feet, with songs and gossip. The deeper the colour, the luckier, so they say.

    3. III

      Sangeet संगीत

      An evening of music and dance, with family and friends performing. Practised, or not practised at all.

    The wedding
    1. IV

      Baraat बारात

      The groom arrives in a procession with his family and friends, with dhol and a great deal of dancing. This is where you naachna.

    2. V

      Varmala वरमाला

      The couple exchange garlands of flowers, a first formal yes to each other in front of everyone.

    3. VI

      Kanyadaan कन्यादान

      The bride's parents entrust their daughter to the groom with their blessings. Often the quietest, most emotional moment of the ceremony.

    4. VII

      Saath Phere सात फेरे

      The couple walk seven times around the sacred fire, each round a vow they make to one another. This is the heart of the wedding.

    5. VIII

      Vidaai विदाई

      The bride leaves her parents' home to begin her new life. Tears are normal, and so is a handful of rice thrown back over her shoulder.
