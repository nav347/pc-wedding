The Plot

Pehle thoda trailer. Volume on rakhiye, ya sirf scroll kijiye.

Scene 01Kuch saal pehle 2015 se

In a world where… college shuru hone se pehle hi ek jodi ban chuki thi.

PC aur Neha. Na koi item number, na koi interval. Bas do log aur ek hi plan. Hum toh baad mein aaye, trailer ka pehla shot unka hai.

Scene 022015, college canteen

Phir aayi COLLEGE GANG. Aur ek banda, jo already taken tha.

Pehle hi mulaqat mein PC ka dialogue: Meri girlfriend hai. Kisi ne poocha bhi nahi tha. Humne uski setting ke saare plan banaye, saare fail. Wajah simple thi: har baar PC ne Neha ki photo nikaal di. Raat ke 11 baje ke baad? Sorry yaar, call pe hoon.

Plot twist
INTERVAL

Neha ek saal ke liye Australia. Popcorn le aao, long distance shuru.

Co-star: WhatsApp video call. Buffering: har emotional scene mein.

PC ne time zone ka maths seekh liya. Good morning aur good night, dono ek hi message mein. Lagbhag.

Scene 03Interval ke baad, second half

And then… ek saal baad, Neha wapas.

Airport pe slow motion nahi tha, par gang ke dil mein poora chal raha tha. Calling plan cancel hua. Good morning ab seedha bol ke. Aur PC? Pehli baar time pe soya.
